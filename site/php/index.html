    §2A.4 Sources

      Every source and note

          The page asks php-feed.php, which reads php.net's supported versions, releases and news feed, php-src's tags, the php.net wiki's timetables, endoflife.date and Packagist, and keeps the merge for ten minutes. If that fails, the page reads endoflife.date and php-src's tags straight from your browser and says so. A source that cannot be read is shown as stale or down; nothing on this page is typed in by hand. php.net wins when sources disagree on a date.

          CREED's dates for the gates stay in policy.json, refreshed on a schedule and reviewed. This page is the live view: it tells you what shipped this morning before the policy file catches up.

          Creed Bratton once claimed to have been in a dozen bands at once. PHP keeps five branches going and at least tells you which ones.
